Practice 1 · Manual verification

Break the answer into claims, then check each one

Citations are a starting point, not proof. Split the response into atomic claims, confirm each against an independent source, and record who cleared it.

Verification Method Tips
1 · Check the sources
Ask for citations, then open them. Confirm the cited text actually says what the answer claims.
2 · Verify exact data points
Cross-reference every number, name, date and legal reference against an authoritative independent source.
3 · Read laterally
Leave the document. Compare each claim with an independent, authoritative source that has no stake in the answer.
Trust, then verify. A cleared claim means a person opened a source and matched it. Nothing on this page clears itself.

Practice 2 · AI Instructions

Constrain the model before you correct it

Lower the temperature for consistency, make the reasoning reviewable, and limit the answer to supplied evidence. AI Instructions compiles your choices into a prompt you can paste into any model.
